Breaking wave slice (illustrative, 2D slice). Simulated along one line from the house (236°), 2.7 m / 7.8 s, tide 2.1 m. One representative wave, no refraction, bed interpolated from 11 × 14 m lidar. Break position good to about ±15–20 m.
Simple model: the swell slows and grows as it shallows, bends toward shore, and is marked broken where height reaches 0.78 x depth.
It does not simulate the shape of a breaking wave.
The surface is interpolated between 11 x 14 m model cells and smoothed for drawing, so it shows no detail finer than the cells. The sea floor stays drawn, dimmer, under the translucent water.
Pale band: rock within about 1 m of the tide level, wet by wave run-up (illustrative, not simulated)
Flat blue: still water at the tide level where the swell model draws nothing (not simulated)
