      Palette

      About

      Reef-scale wave models, one spot at a time. Glowing spots are unlocked: click one to fly in.
      unlocked: reef seabed and wave model
      marked spot, no model yet
      wanted: needs seabed data to unlock
      data needed: coastal hazard and recovery science
      Unlock tiers: marked › seabed › swell model › breaking wave
      Each Earth dot is one point of ETOPO1 global relief (NOAA): ocean blue by depth; land coloured by height (olive low ground, brown uplands, grey-brown high plateaus, paler only on the highest peaks; the Antarctic and Greenland ice sheets blue-grey). Colour shows height, not vegetation or snow.
